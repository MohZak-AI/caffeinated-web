**Motion note**

On desktop, this section stays pinned for 300vh of scrolling. The four lines replace one another while the background and text colors move continuously through the espresso, roasted brown, cinnamon, and dark mocha color stops. Small pooled pixel bubbles appear only at the three color crossings.

On mobile, the four lines appear as a stacked sequence with simple one-time fades. With reduced motion enabled, the lines remain static and fully readable. The pinned scrub and bubbles are disabled in both fallbacks.
