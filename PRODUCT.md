# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary users are founders and local businesses, including cafes and shops,
that need a website built from scratch or an existing site redesigned. They
need a clear scope, an affordable starting point, and ongoing help keeping the
site online after launch.

## Product Purpose

caffienatedweb is a website studio that designs and builds responsive websites
for small businesses and individuals. It also offers an optional monthly care
plan covering hosting, domain management, security, maintenance, and a free
edit every six months. Success is a client receiving a clear plan and quote,
then launching and maintaining a website that supports their business.

## Positioning

The studio presents custom websites with an accessible starting price:
one-time setup starts at $199, followed by an optional low monthly care plan.
It combines a short discovery chat with a simple, usually same-day plan and
fixed quote rather than selling a one-size-fits-all package.

## Operating Context

Prospects browse the marketing site, review the pricing and included services,
then book a 30-minute discovery call through Calendly or contact the studio by
email. Before work begins, the client and studio confirm the scope, timeline,
and price in writing. Clients provide the content, assets, and access needed
to build or redesign their site.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Build new websites and redesign existing websites.
- Offer hosting, domain management, security, and maintenance through an
  optional monthly care plan.
- Starting setup price is $199; each project receives a scoped quote before
  work begins.
- Booking uses a configurable Calendly URL with an email fallback.
- The product must not fabricate studio locations, client case studies,
  customer testimonials, performance statistics, or guarantees.
- Public marketing contact email is `contact@caffienatedweb.com`; legal policy
  contact details should match before launch.
- Marketing copy promises a 15-minute chat, while the current fallback
  Calendly URL points to a 30-minute event and must be replaced before launch.

## Brand Commitments

- Brand name: caffienatedweb.
- Voice: direct, human, energetic, and honest about what is included and what
  still requires a project-specific quote.
- The offer leads with fast, flexible website work and transparent starting
  pricing, not unsupported prestige claims.

## Evidence on Hand

- Landing-page copy and booking configuration: `app/_content/landing.ts`.
- Published terms, privacy, refund, and cookie-policy content:
  `app/_content/legal.ts`.
- Existing product and design decisions: `DESIGN.md`.
- A bundled coffee-themed image asset: `public/caffinated-web.gif`.
- No verified client case studies, testimonials, press coverage, or external
  performance benchmarks are present in the repository and must not be
  invented.

## Product Principles

1. Make the starting price and inclusions clear while scoping every project
   before commitment.
2. Help small businesses move from a website need to a concrete plan through a
   low-friction discovery call.
3. Pair the initial build or redesign with optional ongoing care so clients can
   keep the site maintained after launch.
4. Prefer specific, supportable claims over generic agency prestige language.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The public web experience must be responsive, keyboard-operable, readable, and
respect reduced-motion preferences. Product-specific accessibility standards
beyond those baseline commitments are undecided.
